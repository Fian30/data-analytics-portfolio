# Olist E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Brazilian e-commerce sales performance using the Olist public dataset. The goal is to understand revenue trends, product performance, geographic distribution, and delivery performance to support business decision-making.

## Tools
- Tableau Public
- Microsoft Excel

## Business Questions
- How does revenue change over time?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which states contribute the most to sales?
- How well does the business perform in delivering orders on time?

## Key Metrics
- Total Orders: 97,688
- Total Revenue: 15,846,280
- Delivered Orders: 94,854
- Late Delivery Rate: 8.12%

## Key Insights
- May recorded the highest monthly revenue at 1,718,924.
- São Paulo contributed the highest revenue among the analyzed states at 5,829,852.
- Beauty & Health was among the leading product categories, generating 1,435,190 in revenue.
- Late deliveries represent an opportunity to improve logistics and customer experience.

## Recommendations
- Investigate the factors behind peak-month revenue and use them to inform sales planning.
- Prioritize marketing and sales analysis in high-contributing geographic markets.
- Evaluate delivery performance to identify opportunities to reduce late deliveries.
- Monitor product-category performance to support inventory and promotional decisions.
